About
I'm Wanderson Gandra. I came to software from occupational safety, which probably explains why I care more about whether a system holds up in real operations than whether it looks clever on paper.
Most of my time goes into building products that companies actually depend on: access control, audit trails, documents that can't be lost, numbers people can trust. If a feature doesn't make someone's work safer or simpler, I have a hard time justifying it.

What I'm building
<a href="https://app.sgsseguranca.com.br">
  <img src="./assets/sgs-card.svg" width="100%" alt="SGS: Sistema de Gestão de Segurança" />
</a>
SGS (Sistema de Gestão de Segurança) is a multi-tenant SaaS for occupational health and safety in Brazil. Companies use it to manage regulated documents, run inspections, control who sees what, and track safety indicators across sites. It's in production and used by real clients, not a demo.
Production: https://app.sgsseguranca.com.br
Repository: https://github.com/wandersongandra/sgsseguranca
